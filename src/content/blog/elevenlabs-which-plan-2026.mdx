## The Free Plan — Start Here

The free plan is permanent, not a trial. 10,000 characters per month, no expiry, no credit card required.

10,000 characters is approximately 10 minutes of finished audio. That is enough to:
- Test 6–8 different voices properly (not just the 30-second preview clips)
- Generate a complete short YouTube script in a voice you have auditioned
- Run a voice cloning test if you are on a trial of one of the paid features
- Decide definitively whether ElevenLabs quality is worth paying for

**What the free plan cannot do:** commercial use, voice cloning, or long-form Projects narration. If you need any of those, the free plan will not cover it — but it will tell you whether the voice quality justifies upgrading.

## Starter ($6/month) — Who Actually Needs This?

Starter unlocks three things the free plan does not: commercial use rights, Instant Voice Cloning, and 30,000 characters per month.

The honest case for Starter is narrow. It is right for:
- Someone with a single recurring project (one short video per month, one podcast intro) who does not need Professional Voice Cloning
- A freelancer doing occasional deliverables where commercial rights matter but volume is low

The reason most creators skip Starter and go straight to Creator: **Starter does not include Professional Voice Cloning.** Instant Voice Cloning (IVC), which Starter does include, works from as little as one minute of audio and produces decent results. But Professional Voice Cloning (PVC), which requires Creator tier, is a meaningfully different product — it trains on 30+ minutes of your audio and produces a clone natural enough for public-facing content at scale.

If you are planning to clone your own voice for regular content use, Starter will not give you the quality you need. Creator is the right tier.

## Creator ($22/month) — The Right Plan for Most People

Creator is the plan most serious content creators should be on. Here is exactly what it adds over Starter:

**Professional Voice Cloning (PVC).** This is the reason to upgrade. PVC requires 30+ minutes of clean source audio and produces a voice clone that sounds like a real recording of you — not an approximation. It handles novel sentences, emotional variation, and pacing in a way that IVC simply cannot. If you are building a content brand around your voice — YouTube, podcast, course narration — PVC is the feature that makes ElevenLabs genuinely powerful rather than just convenient.

**121,000 characters per month.** Roughly 2 hours of finished audio. For a creator producing a weekly 10-minute YouTube video, a bi-weekly podcast episode, and occasional short clips, this covers the full output comfortably.

**The Projects feature.** Projects is ElevenLabs' long-form narration tool. Paste a full document — an article, a chapter, a script — and edit at the sentence level. Regenerate a single sentence without affecting the surrounding audio. For audiobooks, course modules, and any content over 5 minutes, Projects is what makes production practically viable rather than tedious.

**Voice Library listing.** Creator and above can list a Professional Voice Clone in the ElevenLabs Voice Library and earn income when other users generate audio using your voice. Some creators earn meaningful passive income from this feature. We cover it in detail in our [Voice Library income guide](/blog/elevenlabs-voice-library-income-2026).

**Is Creator worth $22/month?** For anyone producing regular audio content, yes. If you are recording a 10-minute video script every week in someone else's voice, you are spending time you could reclaim. If you are hiring voice talent for recurring projects, $22 covers the cost of a single short recording. The break-even point is low.

## Pro ($99/month) — When You Have Outgrown Creator

Creator's 121,000 character ceiling becomes a constraint for creators producing high volumes: multiple videos per week, long-form audiobook production, or content pipelines with several contributors.

Pro gives you:
- **600,000 characters per month** — roughly 10 hours of audio
- **Expanded API access** — higher rate limits and additional capabilities for automated workflows and application integrations
- **Usage-based billing** — you can go over your monthly allowance and pay per character rather than being hard-stopped

**Who needs Pro:** Growing teams, moderate-volume production pipelines, developers building ElevenLabs into applications, and any creator whose monthly usage regularly hits Creator's ceiling.

**Who does not need Pro yet:** Most individual creators. If you are not regularly using 100,000+ characters per month on Creator, Pro is premature. Upgrade when Creator becomes a bottleneck — not before.

## Scale ($330/month) and Enterprise

Scale provides 2,000,000 characters per month and is designed for high-volume businesses, content platforms, and enterprise integrations. Enterprise adds custom terms, SLA-backed support, and volume pricing.

If you are evaluating Scale or Enterprise, you already know whether you need it. The upgrade path from Pro is clear: when 600,000 characters per month is not enough, Scale is the next step.
